Four heat exchanger types, built to order

Each type suits a different airflow, temperature and space requirement. If you’re not sure which one fits, we’ll choose for you.

Cross-flow

Compact air-to-air exchangers for ventilation and exhaust heat recovery where space is limited.

Counter-flow

Opposite-direction airflow for higher heat recovery efficiency in energy-saving systems.

Runner (rotary)

Rotating wheel exchangers for large air volumes and continuous heat recovery.

Heat pipe

Sealed, passive heat transfer with no moving parts, suited to flue gas and harsh exhaust streams.
